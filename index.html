About

I am a Research Engineer at BharatGen, where I co-lead the vision effort. My work sits at the intersection of vision–language models, document understanding, and ML systems.

I built Patram-7B-Instruct — a 7B VLM trained from scratch on diverse Indian documents spanning 23 languages, scaling to 512 GPUs via a modular training framework. I am the first author of M3Grounder (CVPR 2026), a pixel-level grounded document QA system, and have 2 papers accepted at ICDAR 2026 and 2 under review at ECCV 2026.

Alongside research, I am actively learning the systems side: I am building CUDA kernels from scratch (SGEMM, Flash Attention) and implementing a minimal inference engine (tiny-infer) to deeply understand LLM inference scheduling, KV-cache management, and optimization.

I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering at IIIT Hyderabad (2021–2025, 8.88 CGPA).

CVPR 2026 CORE A* · 1st Author
M3Grounder: Mask-Based Multi-Span and Multi-Granular Grounding for Document QA
Venkata Kesav Venna, Sai Madhusudan Gunda, Jyothi Swaroopa Jinka, Hrithik Sagar Rachakonda, Anirudh Srinivasan, Ravi Kiran Sarvadevabhatla
Novel hybrid VLM–segmentation architecture that grounds answer spans at phrase, line, and block granularities via pixel-level masks. Introduces a bleed-suppression loss and multi-span grounding, achieving state-of-the-art on 4 benchmarks (F1g: 66.4 → 73.3 on DOGR-Bench at 8B scale). Released GroundingDocQA (200K docs, 2M QA pairs) and GroundingDocQA-Bench (2.5K human-verified).

Venkata Kesav Venna et al.

Projects
tiny-infer
C++ · CUDA · 2026 · 60-day Challenge
Building a minimal but real CUDA inference engine for Llama 3.2 1B from scratch in 60 days (1 hr/day). Week 1–2: correctness (forward pass matching HF). Week 3: custom kernels (Flash Attention, fused RMSNorm/RoPE). Week 4: paged KV cache. Week 5–6: speculative decoding. Week 7–8: INT8 KV quantization. Every day moves a number; every optimization benchmarked. Target: 40 tok/s vs 5 tok/s baseline. Repo ↗

GPU Kernel Series
CUDA C/C++ · 2026 – Present · Ongoing
9 CUDA kernels built from scratch: vector add → SGEMM progression (naive → coalesced → tiled shared-memory → 1D register-tiled), each benchmarked for warp-level memory access patterns and arithmetic intensity. Implemented naive O(N²) attention and Flash Attention forward pass, fusing score, online softmax, and output accumulation into a single kernel pass — reducing HBM complexity from O(N²) to O(N).
